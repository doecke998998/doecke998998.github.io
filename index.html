Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private tool I use to publish videos to my own YouTube channels:

- Sleepy Scripture
- Drowsy History for Sleep
- Ember and Rain Sleep Sounds
- Money Made Simple Budgeting

It runs on my own computer. It uploads my finished videos with their titles, descriptions,
thumbnails and publish times, and posts a first comment on my own videos linking to the
full-length version. It is not a public service and has no other users.

It uses the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms)
and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Read the Privacy Policy page for how data is handled.
