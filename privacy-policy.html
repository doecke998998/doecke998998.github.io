Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Channel Uploader ("the app") is a private, internal tool. It is used only by its owner to upload
and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels: Sleepy Scripture, Drowsy History for
Sleep, Ember and Rain Sleep Sounds, and Money Made Simple Budgeting. It is not offered to the
public and has no other users.

What the app accesses
When the owner authorises it, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:
- upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, category, thumbnails, privacy and scheduled publish time;
- post comments on the owner's own videos;
- read basic details of the owner's own channels (channel name, ID, list of uploads and the
  comments on the owner's videos) to confirm the right channel is connected and to avoid
  uploading or commenting twice.

What the app does not do
- It does not collect, store or analyse any information about viewers, commenters, subscribers or other third parties.
- It does not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to anyone.
- It does not use any data for advertising, analytics or profiling.
- It has no user accounts, cookies or tracking.

How data is stored
The Google access tokens that let the app act on the owner's channels are stored locally on the
owner's own computer, in files that only the owner can read. They are only ever sent to Google
when the app calls the YouTube API. The video files, thumbnails and text the owner uploads stay
on the same computer and are sent only to YouTube. Simple activity logs (for example, "video
uploaded at 9:00") are kept locally and contain no tokens or personal information.

How to revoke access
Access can be withdrawn at any time: go to your Google Account, then Security, then "Your
connections to third-party apps and services" (https://myaccount.google.com/connections), choose
Channel Uploader and select "Delete all connections". Deleting the local token files also stops
the app working straight away.

Google and YouTube
The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data
Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited
Use requirements. Using the app also means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service
(https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's own handling of data is explained in the Google
Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Changes
